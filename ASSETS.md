# Moscow vehicle photographs and customization

The built-in `image_gen` tool generated four production texture atlases for 38 import models and 28 enthusiast model families. Runtime assets: `img/urban/import-moscow-1.webp`, `img/urban/import-moscow-2.webp`, `img/urban/legends-moscow-1.webp`, `img/urban/legends-moscow-2.webp`. Original PNGs were copied from generated output and encoded as WebP at unchanged dimensions for web delivery. Each atlas is a single generated game asset. Cars are shown in stock silver paint on Moscow streets; these are game model illustrations, not evidence of a real vehicle's condition, equipment or location.

The exact final prompt set and built-in mode are recorded in `data/urban-generation-prompts.json`. `data/vehicle-visuals.json` maps every model to its own divider-free atlas rectangle, body/window polygons and two wheel positions. `python3 scripts/build-vehicle-visuals.py` validates original asset hashes and annotations and compiles `vehicle-visuals.js`. The built-in tool also traced four registered RGB annotation textures in `img/urban/masks/` (red paint, green glass, blue rims). `scripts/trace-vehicle-masks.py` converts those technical textures into native polygon geometry, aligns them to original photo edges and records the transform without editing any photograph; its rebuild-only dependencies are Pillow, NumPy and OpenCV. Runtime SVG masks recolour only painted panels, darken windows and place code-native rim artwork at the model's measured wheel positions. Raster assets are never overwritten by a player's choices. Factory colour and installed parts are saved separately from immutable signed documents. Previous supplier illustrations below remain for archive data and attribution.

## Private-market image audit and reviewed frames

All 837 current private-market photos were checked for existence, decoding and contact-sheet slicing artefacts. The 87 corrections in `data/market-photo-crops.json` remove divider lines and fragments of neighbouring panels at display time. Original raster files are untouched. Each reviewed entry records source dimensions and SHA-256; `python3 scripts/build-market-photo-crops.py` validates the originals and compiles the small `market-photo-crops.js` browser lookup. The same proportional SVG frame is used in cards, detailed views and the garage. Cosmetic wear overlays remain separate.

## Catalogue framing and model labels

Dealer frames use a 3:2 aspect ratio and proportional SVG fill, removing side bars without stretching the vehicle shape. Import drawings retain their fitted framing. Import titles show the stored vehicle year again; the NX 300h (2.5 hybrid) and UX 250h (2.0 hybrid) suffixes are sourced from Lexus manufacturer press releases linked in `commerce-models.json`. Existing stock keys, car IDs, prices and archived contract snapshots are unchanged.

The built-in `image_gen` tool was used for the two cosmetic-wear assets. The original generated PNGs were retained outside the repository; the game uses optimised WebP copies.

- `img/wear/lada-2114.webp`: edited from `img/fictional/1.jpg`; same silver car and courtyard, with cosmetic scuffs and light rust. After preparation the game returns to the original clean photograph.

Prompt for the Lada edit:

> Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: the attached local photo of a silver Lada 2114, a cheap used car in a Russian car trading game. Add plausible visible age-related wear ONLY on the car: scuffed front bumper, a few scratches in paint on the lower door and fender, small rust spots along sill and wheel arch, slightly faded paint. Keep the exact same car model, silver color, shape, camera angle, wheels, courtyard and light, car fully intact and roadworthy. Natural used-car listing photograph, subtle realistic wear, no crash damage, no overlays or text. This car costs about 90,000 roubles, make its cosmetic wear clear without exaggeration.

Prompt for the transparent overlay:

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: transparent cosmetic wear texture for compositing on a car body in a browser game. A square transparent canvas containing ONLY a few thin pale paint scuffs, dusty grey surface scratches, two small muted rust flecks and narrow scraped paint streaks. Arrange these sparse markings across a horizontal band from 25% to 80% of the image width, around 48% to 74% of its height. Subtle wear of an old cheap used car, not crash damage. Actual alpha transparency.

## Model photographs

`img/commerce/*.webp` (except the generated import atlas described above) contains locally cached model photographs from Wikimedia Commons. Every image has an adjacent JSON record containing the original file, source URL, author, original licence and licence URL. The only processing was reduction in size and conversion to WebP. Original licences apply. `commerce-photo-credits.html` exposes the same attribution in the game.

The active catalogue contains one car per model: 49 new dealer models and 38 import models. The former 1,000 stock IDs are retained solely for compatibility with saved cars and orders. Photographs illustrate the model; the selected colour, equipment and condition are stated separately. Prices, equipment bundles, stock and import charges are game data.

Russian dealer selection was informed by official model ranges, including:

- https://haval.ru/
- https://www.chery.ru/models/
- https://www.geely-motors.com/
- https://jaecoo.ru/models/
- https://jetour-ru.com/
- https://livan-motors.ru/model/x3pro/

`commerce-models.json` is the curated source, `scripts/fetch-commerce-photos.py` refreshes the local photograph cache, and `scripts/build-commerce-models.py` compiles the browser data and attribution page. The game does not fetch catalogue data or photographs from third-party sites at runtime.
